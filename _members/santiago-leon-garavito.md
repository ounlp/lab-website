Santiago León Garavito is an undergraduate at the University of Oklahoma double majoring in Economics and Applied Artificial Intelligence. He joined the OUNLP Lab in December 2025 and works with Dr. Jie Cao on music information retrieval. His focus is how audio AI models handle instruments from outside the Western canon.

His first lab project compared two audio classification models, CLAP and PANNs, on musical instrument detection using AudioSet. He presented it through the First-Year Research Experience (FYRE). That work pointed to a gap: the models had trouble recognizing instruments that are rare in their training data, and many of those are non-Western. With a 2026 UROP award, he is now building an open, tradition-annotated dataset of Chinese, Andean, and Middle Eastern instrument recordings. He will use it to measure that gap instrument by instrument and test how much of it comes from missing data.

Santiago grew up in Colombia and studied at UWC Dilijan in Armenia before coming to OU. He plays guitar and bass in several bands. He is interested in work that connects technology and the arts, including the economics of creative industries.
